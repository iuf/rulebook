\section{Definition}

In IUF competitions, unicycle basketball is played using the international FIBA rules for on-foot basketball with the few changes explained in this chapter.
The international FIBA rules to be used are the currently valid ones, for regular basketball (\url{https://www.fiba.basketball/documents})
or for 3x3 basketball (\url{https://fiba3x3.com/en/documents.html#rules}), depending on the event.
At Unicon, at least a regular 5x5 unicycle basketball event must be proposed.

\section{Rider Summary}

This section is intended as an overview of the rules, but does not substitute for the actual rules.
\begin{itemize}
\item Be aware that the following rules augment the international rules of basketball.
\item You must wear shoes as defined in chapter \ref{chap:general_definitions}.
\item All team players must wear shirts of the same color.
\item Be aware of the unicycle restrictions for basketball.
\end{itemize}
